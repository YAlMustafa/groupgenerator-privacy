Group Generator – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Group Generator ("the app") is made by QuarksY. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. In short: the app does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your device, in its own private storage:

  • Your settings: language, mode, number of players and groups, and options.
  • Any member names and group names you type.
  • The history of your draws: date and time, settings, names and results.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and the developer cannot see it. You can delete draws in the app's History screen (one at a time or all at once), and you can remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Device backup

If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup (for example, your Google account backup), under Google's terms. The developer has no access to these backups.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising tools. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Donations

Donations are optional and are processed entirely by Google Play. The app only learns whether a donation succeeded; it never receives your payment details. Google's handling of payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing results

When you tap Share, the app hands the results text to Android's share menu, and you choose where it goes. The app does not send it anywhere by itself.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
